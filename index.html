About the Lab

Integrative planetary surface science.

We connect laboratory environmental simulations with satellite imagery to test process-based hypotheses.

Led by Dr. Trishit Ruj at Okayama University, PGSSL bridges environmental chamber testing with planetary observations.

Our research encompasses Martian brine stability, cold-climate periglacial landforms, paleohydrologic deltas, lunar crustal tectonics, and machine learning application for planetary mapping.

Chamber Experiments

Simulating pressure, ice stability, and salt deliquescence.

Field Analogs

Studying Earth terrestrial environments to understand Mars geology.

Orbital Remote Sensing

Spectral and geomorphic mapping from spacecraft datasets.

Research Areas

Key scientific investigations.

01

Martian Brine Stability

Deliquescence, transient water liquid formation, and hydration pathways under Mars atmospheric conditions.

02

Periglacial Geomorphology

Polygonal ground, ground ice, and cold-climate surface modifications on Martian mid-to-high latitudes.

03

Delta Morphodynamics

Sedimentation tank experiments to reconstruct ancient lake and oceanic shoreline dynamics on Mars.

04

AI Planetary Mapping

Deep learning models for automated geomorphic feature detection and landform classification.

05

Lunar Tectonics

Structural geological analysis of lunar crustal stresses and surface exploration hazards.

06

Lab-to-Orbit Spectral Linking

Integrating VNIR/SWIR hyperspectral imaging with satellite spectrometer observations.
